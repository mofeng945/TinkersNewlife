What was taken from it (all of it parameter/formula level, re-implemented in this mod's own code):

* `TrainerStylePrecisionGuide`: the sweep half angle (68 degrees), the sweep start/span in progress
  (0.30 / 0.34), the attack duration (0.5 s = 10 ticks), and the per-segment follow delay (0.24 s).
* `LeatherWhipPhysics`: the hand-base anchor formula (feet + eye height - 0.58, + 0.34 to the right,
  + 0.10 forward), the acceleration curve `pow(t, 1.55)` of the stroke, and the rope parameters
  (gravity -21.5, velocity retention 0.989, max stretch 1.003, 5 solver iterations).
* The left/right alternating swing direction of consecutive lashes.
