# Tinker's Newlife 1.0.1.27 — The Starwell Arena

Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · Changes since 1.0.1.26

## Find the arena and summon Momo

- The Starwell Arena can now generate on the cliffs of large outer End islands in newly generated chunks. Its entrance faces the island, while the rear points of its eight-pointed foundation extend over the void. The terraced, 48-block-radius altar is surrounded by stands and columns, with fractured orbital rings overhead and a vault beneath the floor.
- In the End, use `/locate structure tinkersnewlife:starwell_arena` to search for an arena. Added Chinese structure names for the arena and the Great White Space variant of Sleepwake Church.
- Once the Ender Dragon has been defeated at least once in that world, finish eating **R'lyeh's Call** within 20 blocks of the center of a naturally generated arena. The ritual completes the beacon base; a blast and expanding sonic ring push players back before Momo appears at the center. Pasting the template alone does not register a ritual site.
- A new boss Momo cannot spawn within 256 blocks of another in the same dimension. Tracked bosses retain their place when their chunks unload. Merchant Mo Mo no longer targets boss Momo, even when hired.

## Fight and rewards

- Momo's **Star Dream** ultimate locks onto the target's position at the start of a 10-second chant. A growing black hole pulls enemies inward beneath a golden hexagram. A 5-block-radius beam then strikes for 5 seconds, applying ten ordinary damage types for 3 base damage each per tick before defenses. The black hole now refracts the scene at its rim, and the chant's arm pose has been corrected.
- Momo retaliates against attackers instead of immediately losing them to the nearest-player scan. When melee hits repeatedly fail to deal damage, a target is airborne, or a route cannot be found, she can switch to ranged combat and move around the target rather than closing for melee.
- Discordant Chord now sends **5 waves**, spaced **2.25–4.5 seconds** apart. R'lyeh's Shadows assist their owner, have 6 base armor, and can be summoned again after 120 seconds while Momo is at or below half health and the previous shadow shield is down. Domain squid have 3 base armor and spawn with one quarter of Momo's maximum health.
- After Momo's death explosion inside the arena, look around the **underground beacon** for 3–5 reward chests, space permitting. Each chest rolls 5–15 times for materials, relics, End loot, and a chance at one flying sword made entirely from tier 2 or higher materials. **Empty the chests before the next victory:** the next kill replaces the previous reward chests and their contents; player-placed containers are left alone.
