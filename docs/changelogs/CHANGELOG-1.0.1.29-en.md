# Tinker's Newlife 1.0.1.29

- **Rapier:** Armor piercing applies only to direct melee hits made with a rapier. Armor wear, Resistance, Protection, and absorption still apply. Holding a rapier no longer gives unrelated projectiles armor piercing.
- **New materials:** Pixie Silver, Crystallized Dragonbone, and Dread Soulsteel, with their own parts, traits, and crafting or casting recipes. Dread Soulsteel now uses the blue-tempered texture set.
- **Pixie Silver:** Carried pixie jars grant boons only at three or more combined Friend of Pixies levels.
- **Fluids:** Added Mantle unit support for metals, gems, and bottled fluids. Filled in missing bucket tags and fixed a loading crash affecting Crystal Essence and Molten Sapphire textures.
- **Cosmetics:** Updated the Yanying fufu skin.
